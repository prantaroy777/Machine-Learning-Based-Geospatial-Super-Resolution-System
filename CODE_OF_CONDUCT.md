# Community expectations

Contributors and users should communicate respectfully, welcome newcomers,
describe technical disagreements with evidence, and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull
requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and consistent.
To raise a concern, use a private maintainer contact if one is published, or ask
for a private contact without reposting abusive or sensitive material. GitHub's
platform reporting tools are also available.
